# Overview

## Purpose
StackForge is designed to accelerate engineering velocity by acting as an on-demand DevOps, engineering, and systems administration co-pilot. By helping internal team members troubleshoot build failures, automate infrastructure setups, and optimize deployment flows, StackForge reduces cognitive load for developers.

## Scope of Impact
- Accelerates onboarding for new team members handling infrastructure.
- Shortens the MTTR (Mean Time to Resolution) for pipeline and environment failures.
- Promotes standard DevOps practices across different service architectures.
